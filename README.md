# ST. Frame & Truss Co., Ltd. — Official Corporate Website

Official bilingual corporate website and engineering portfolio for **ST. Frame & Truss Co., Ltd.** — Thailand's premier structural steel fabrication and erection specialist.

🌐 **Live Website (GitHub Pages):** [https://bpa111111.github.io/stframe-website/](https://bpa111111.github.io/stframe-website/)

---

## 🏗️ Highlights & Features

- **Turnkey Bilingual Architecture:** 100% synchronized dynamic Thai / English language switcher (`main.js`).
- **ST Magazine & Engineering Catalog:** Complete interactive archives from 2018 to 2026 with PDF viewer and instant search.
- **Media & Video Gallery:** Corporate documentaries, 3D BIM showcase, landmark projects, and CSR roadshow videos.
- **Featured & Landmark Projects:** 42+ reference projects across industrial plants, commercial megaprojects, and national heritage structures.
- **Interactive Contact & Careers:** Dynamic job application form with CV/Resume upload and exact Google Maps integration (Ayutthaya plant).
- **WordPress Theme Compatibility:** Pre-packaged custom WordPress theme included in `stframe-theme/`.

```
├── careers.html                # Job Openings & Internship Application
├── contact.html                # Contact Us & Google Maps Plant Location

```
